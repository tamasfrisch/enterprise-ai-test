# Business Continuity Policy
Document ID: OPS-020 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Approved | Effective: 1 January 2026

Business owners must identify critical services, dependencies, recovery priorities, and minimum operating requirements. Each critical service must have a documented continuity plan reviewed annually and after major operational changes.

During a disruption, the incident lead coordinates response, records decisions, and updates affected stakeholders. Recovery objectives are set by the service owner and approved through the continuity process; this policy does not define a single recovery time for every system.

Critical suppliers and data dependencies must be included in continuity planning. Lessons from exercises and incidents must be assigned to owners with target completion dates.
