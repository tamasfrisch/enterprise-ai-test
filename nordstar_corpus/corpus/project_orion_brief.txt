# Project Orion — Project Brief
Document ID: PM-016 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Approved | Date: 15 January 2026

Project Orion integrates operational telemetry from selected manufacturing sites into Nordstar's Atlas Analytics platform. The approved 2026 project budget is EUR 4.2 million.

The pilot target date is 30 September 2026. The pilot validates data ingestion, data-quality monitoring, and operational dashboards at initial participating sites. The Chief Operating Officer is the project sponsor. The project manager maintains the plan, risks, dependencies, and status reports.

Any forecast increase above the approved budget must be raised with the sponsor and Finance. Scope or target-date changes must be documented in the change log and approved through project governance.
