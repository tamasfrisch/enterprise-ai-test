# Remote Work Policy
Document ID: HR-004 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Approved | Effective: 1 January 2026

Eligible employees may work remotely up to four days per working week, subject to role requirements, team coordination, and manager agreement. Employees must remain reachable during agreed working hours and protect company information regardless of location.

Remote work from another country requires prior written approval from Human Resources and the employee's manager because tax, employment, security, and immigration considerations may apply. Employees must use approved devices and secure connectivity.

Managers may require on-site attendance for operational, customer, training, or security reasons. This policy does not create an entitlement to work remotely from any particular location.
