# Supplier Qualification Policy
Document ID: PROC-012 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Approved | Effective: 1 January 2026

New suppliers must undergo identity, financial, and sanctions screening before onboarding. Suppliers accessing Nordstar systems, processing company or customer data, or supporting critical operations require a risk assessment before approval.

The assessment considers information security, privacy, business continuity, financial stability, geographic exposure, and subcontractor use. High-risk suppliers require Procurement approval and sign-off from relevant control owners, such as Information Security or Data Protection.

Qualification does not replace contract review. Material changes to services, ownership, data access, or subcontractors trigger reassessment. Procurement maintains the supplier record and most recent review date.
