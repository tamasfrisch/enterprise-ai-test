# Warranty and Maintenance Policy
Document ID: SVC-015 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Approved | Effective: 1 January 2026

Standard hardware warranty is 12 months from customer acceptance unless the signed customer contract specifies otherwise. Warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use; misuse, unauthorized modification, or unsupported environmental conditions are excluded.

Preventive maintenance schedules depend on product type, operating environment, and service package. Maintenance visits must be recorded in ServiceLink. Staff must not promise a warranty extension or repair completion date without confirmation from the service owner and applicable contract.

Where a contract conflicts with this policy, the signed contract governs the customer-specific commitment.
