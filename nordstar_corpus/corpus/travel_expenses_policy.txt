# Travel and Expenses Policy
Document ID: FIN-TRV-002 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Approved | Effective: 1 January 2026

Employees must obtain manager approval before booking business travel. Use the approved travel channel where available. Economy class is the default for flights shorter than six hours. For flights of six hours or longer, premium economy may be booked with department manager approval.

Hotel costs should be reasonable for the destination and business purpose. Itemized receipts are required for reimbursable expenses. Expense claims should be submitted within 30 calendar days after the trip ends. Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and accompanying family members' expenses are not reimbursable.

If a receipt is unavailable, the employee must explain why and obtain manager approval. Finance may request supporting evidence.
