# Product Portfolio Overview
Document ID: PROD-014 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Approved | Effective: 1 January 2026

Nordstar's fictional products:
- Sentinel Monitor: industrial equipment monitoring and alerting.
- FlowControl Edge: edge gateway for collecting equipment telemetry.
- Atlas Analytics: analytics and reporting platform for operational data.
- ServiceLink: customer support and maintenance coordination portal.

Sentinel Monitor detects configured thresholds and sends alerts. FlowControl Edge collects telemetry from compatible devices and forwards approved data to platform services. Atlas Analytics provides dashboards and trend analysis; results depend on source-data quality. ServiceLink manages support requests, service schedules, and customer-visible case updates.

Availability, compatibility, warranty, and support commitments must be checked against current product documentation and the customer's signed contract.
