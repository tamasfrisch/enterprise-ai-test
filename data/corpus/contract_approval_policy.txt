# Contract Review and Approval Policy
Document ID: LEG-003 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Approved | Effective: 1 January 2026

All contracts creating a commercial obligation must be reviewed by Legal before signature, except standard low-risk purchase orders covered by an approved template and procurement process.

Contracts above EUR 25,000 require Finance review. Contracts involving personal data, confidential customer information, or production-system access require Information Security and Data Protection review. Contracts longer than three years require Legal Director approval.

Only employees listed in the current Delegation of Authority register may sign contracts. Purchase approval does not itself authorize an employee to sign a contract.
