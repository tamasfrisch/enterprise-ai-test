# United States Remote Work Addendum
Document ID: HR-US-005 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Approved | Effective: 1 January 2026
Scope: Employees whose assigned work location is in the United States.

Eligible US-based employees may work remotely up to four days per working week, consistent with the global Remote Work Policy. Managers may require more frequent on-site attendance for business needs.

This addendum does not authorize work from a different country. Cross-border remote work still requires prior written approval from Human Resources and the employee's manager. Where this addendum is silent, the global policy applies.
